# 🛡️ CrimeShield

## Smart Citizen–Police Crime Reporting & Safety Platform

CrimeShield is a web-based platform designed to improve communication between citizens and law enforcement authorities. It provides citizens with an accessible digital platform to report crimes, track complaints, access emergency assistance, and obtain important legal information.

The platform also provides a dedicated interface for police personnel to manage and review reported complaints.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 👤 Citizen Portal

- 🔐 Citizen Login
- 📊 Citizen Dashboard
- 📝 Online Crime Reporting
- 🔎 Complaint Status Tracking
- 🚨 Emergency SOS
- 📷 Camera Access for Incident Capture
- ⚖️ Citizen Laws & Legal Information
- 📞 Emergency & Support Contacts

### 👮 Police Portal

- 🔐 Police Login
- 📊 Police Dashboard
- 📋 View Reported Complaints
- 📝 File & Manage Complaints
- 🔎 Review Crime Reports
- 👤 Access Citizen Complaint Information

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Make crime reporting easier and more accessible
- Provide citizens with a digital platform for submitting complaints
- Enable complaint status tracking
- Provide quick access to emergency assistance
- Improve communication between citizens and police
- Centralize crime-related information
- Make complaint management more organized and transparent
